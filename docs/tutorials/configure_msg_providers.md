# Configure Messaging Providers

Messaging providers offer access to communication platforms such as WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Slack, and more.
This tutorial shows how to choose a channel and add its provider to your team.
Participants can then chat with your chatbot on the platform they already use.

If you want a Chat Widget on a website, you don't need to set up a messaging provider.
See [details on the Chat Widget](../chat_widget/index.md).

## Steps

### 1) Choose the communication channel
Choose one of the supported [messaging providers](../concepts/team/messaging_providers.md).

### 2) Create an account with the messaging service provider

Before configuring a provider, ensure that you have an active account at the provider and access to the necessary integration credentials.

### 3) Add the messaging provider details to your Team

Go to **Team Settings → [Integrations](../concepts/team/integrations.md)**.
Click **Add integration** and choose your provider from the **Messaging** group.

For provider-specific setup, see:

- [WhatsApp](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#whatsapp)
- [Telegram](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#telegram)
- [WhatsApp Business Platform with Meta](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#meta-cloud-api)
- [Slack](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#slack)
- [Facebook Messenger](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#facebook-messenger)
- [SureAdhere secure In-App Messaging](../how-to/channels/deploy_to_different_channels.md#sureadhere-for-secure-in-app-messaging)
